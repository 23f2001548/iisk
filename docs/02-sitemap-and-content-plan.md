## 2. Page-by-Page Content Plan

### Home Page (Single Page Layout)
* **Hero Section**: High-quality campus image, School Name, Tagline ("Empowering Minds, Shaping Futures"), and a primary "Enquire Now" CTA.
* **Quick Stats**: CBSE Affiliation (1731086), Established 2018, Classes (Play Group - 12).
* **About Us & Principal's Welcome**: Short quote and vision statement.
* **Academics & Facilities**: Highlights like technology-integrated classrooms and labs.
* **Testimonials**: "What Parents Say" section featuring parent reviews and ratings.
* **Admissions**: Step-by-step process and required documents list.
* **Social Presence**: Embedded live Instagram Feed via Elfsight.
* **Contact Us**: Address, Phone, Email, Enquiry form, and interactive Google Map pin.
* **Footer**: Address, Phone, Email, Map link, quick links.

### About Us
* **Vision & Mission**: The core philosophy of the school.
* **Principal's Message**: Full message from Principal Durgesh Nandini Saini [Verify].
* **History**: Brief timeline since 2018 [Verify].

### Academics
* **Curriculum**: CBSE Board, Senior Secondary details.
* **Methodology**: Personalised guidance, classroom technology.
* **Classes Offered**: Break down by groups (Pre-Primary, Primary, Middle, Secondary, Senior Secondary).

### Facilities & Activities
* **Infrastructure**: Details on labs, library, smart classrooms.
* **Co-curricular**: Sports, arts, cultural events.
* *(Combined into one page to reduce click depth for users).*

### Admissions
* **Process overview**: Step-by-step guide (e.g., Visit School -> Fill Form -> Interaction).
* **Required Documents**: List of documents needed (Birth certificate, Aadhar, Transfer Certificate, etc.).
* **Call to Action**: Enquiry form embedded or link to Contact page.

### Gallery
* **Photo Grid**: Real photos of the campus, classrooms, events, and students. (No stock photos).

### Mandatory Public Disclosure
* **CBSE Compliance**: A simple list of required PDF links (Affiliation letter, Society Registration, NOC, Fire Safety, Water/Health certificates, Fee structure, Academic Calendar, SMC details).

### Contact Us
* **Information**: Address (Kaithun Road, Raipura, Kota, Rajasthan, 325001 - near Aryan College), Phone numbers, Emails.
* **Interactive Map**: Google Maps embed.
* **Enquiry Form**: Name, Phone, Class of interest, Message.
