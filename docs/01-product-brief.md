# Indira International School - Product Brief

## 1. Product Goals
* **Primary Objective**: Drive more admission enquiries and provide clear, accessible school information to prospective and current parents.
* **Secondary Objective**: Establish a professional, modern digital presence that reflects the school's commitment to quality education ("Empowering Minds, Shaping Futures").
* **Maintenance Goal**: Keep it lightweight, fast, and extremely easy for school staff to maintain without technical expertise.

## 2. Target Users
* Parents and guardians in and around Kota, Rajasthan (specifically Raipura / Kaithun Road area).
* Mostly accessing the internet via mobile phones on average mobile networks (3G/4G).
* Prefer direct communication (WhatsApp/Phone) over complex forms.

## 3. User Personas

### Persona 1: The Busy Local Parent (Rahul)
* **Profile**: Working professional, relies heavily on mobile.
* **Needs**: Quick access to contact information, school location (Google Maps), and a fast way to ask a question (WhatsApp or short form).
* **Pain Points**: Slow-loading websites, buried phone numbers, forced account creation.
* **Key Actions**: Clicking to call, opening the map, scanning the Home page for basic class details (Play Group to 12th).

### Persona 2: The Research-Oriented Parent (Priya)
* **Profile**: Planning their child's long-term education, compares multiple schools.
* **Needs**: Verification of CBSE affiliation (No. 1731086), details about infrastructure, teaching methodology, principal's background, and co-curricular activities.
* **Pain Points**: Lack of transparency, generic stock photos instead of real campus images, missing Mandatory Public Disclosures.
* **Key Actions**: Reading the About Us page, reviewing Academics and Facilities, checking the Admissions process.

## 4. MVP Scope (Phase 1 Build)
* Fast, mobile-first informational static website.
* Clear display of school details, affiliation, and contact info.
* Simple Enquiry Form (Name, Phone, Class of interest, Message) that routes directly to the school's email.
* WhatsApp click-to-chat and Click-to-call buttons.
* Dual language support consideration (English with a plan for a Hindi toggle).
* Clean, professional UI adhering to modern design principles but avoiding heavy interactive bloat.

## 5. Non-Goals (Out of Scope for First Version)
* NO fee payment gateway or processing.
* NO parent/student/staff login portals.
* NO online attendance tracking or results management system.
* NO comprehensive online admission application forms (only a simple lead-gen enquiry form).
* NO complex Content Management System (CMS) that requires a database.
